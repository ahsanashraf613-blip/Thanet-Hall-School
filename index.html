Our Journey

From a small institution to a thriving campus.

Thanet Hall School opened its doors in Sabzazar in 1998 with a vision to provide quality, value-based education to the local community. What began as a small institution has grown into one of the most respected schools in the area.

Through every stage of growth, we have remained anchored to our founding conviction: that every child deserves an education that honors their individuality while demanding their best.

Academics

A curriculum that challenges and inspires.

Four distinct academic stages, each thoughtfully designed to meet students where they are and propel them toward who they can become.

Playgroup - Prep

Early Years Program

Our Early Years program provides a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where young children begin their educational journey. Through play-based learning, storytelling, and hands-on activities, we develop language, motor skills, and social-emotional foundations.

  • Play-based, child-centered pedagogy
  • Phonics and early literacy foundations
  • Dedicated early-years activity room
  • Safe and colorful learning environment
Class 1 - 5

Primary School

The primary years are where curiosity meets discipline. Our students master core literacy and numeracy while exploring science, social studies, and Islamic studies. We emphasize reading habits, mental mathematics, and inquiry-based learning.

  • Core subjects aligned with national curriculum
  • Daily reading and library periods
  • Computer literacy from Class 3
  • Nazra Quran and Islamic etiquettes
Class 6 - 8

Middle School

As students enter adolescence, our program grows more rigorous and reflective. Specialist teachers guide them through advanced mathematics, laboratory sciences, and analytical writing. Critical thinking and collaboration become explicit goals.

  • Subject-specialist teaching faculty
  • Physics, chemistry, and biology labs
  • Pre-Matric stream selection guidance
  • Public speaking and debate program
Class 9 - 10

Secondary Matric

Our secondary program prepares students for the Punjab Board Matriculation examinations with structured rigor. Students choose between Science and Computer Science groups, receiving focused coaching and past-paper practice that has produced consistent top-tier results.

  • Science and Computer Science groups
  • Intensive board examination preparation
  • Career counseling and college guidance
  • 100% pass rate in recent boards

Admissions 2025-2026

Begin your journey with us.

A clear, supportive admission process designed to help your family make the right choice with confidence.

How to Apply

Four simple steps to enrollment.

01

Inquiry

Submit the online inquiry form or visit our admissions office to receive the prospectus.

02

Assessment

Age-appropriate entrance evaluation in English, Mathematics, and Urdu for Class 1 onwards.

03

Interview

A friendly conversation with parents and the student to understand aspirations and fit.

04

Enrollment

Receive offer of admission, submit required documents, and complete fee formalities.

Required Documents

What to bring.

  • Completed Admission Form
    Available at the admissions office.
  • 4 Passport Photographs
    Recent, with white background.
  • B-Form / Birth Certificate
    NADRA-issued, original and photocopy.
  • School Leaving Certificate
    From previous school (for Class 2 onwards).
  • Last Report Card
    Most recent academic record.
